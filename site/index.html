Questions

Which iPhones does it work on?

Any iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Reading Arabic in scans uses Apple's text recognition, which needs iOS 18 or later.

Can I edit any PDF?

PDFs with real text can be edited directly. Scanned pages and photos are first read into text, then edited the same way. Password-protected PDFs open with their password, within what their owner allows.

Is a signature in PDF Editor legally binding?

It places an image of your handwritten signature on the page, like signing a printout. Whether that is enough depends on the document and where you are; ask the person who sent it if you are unsure.

How do I cancel?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose PDF Editor Pro. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
